Case Study

Proof of work.

Case Study · 01
How I delivered conflict-free interview scheduling for a full recruitment cycle.
ClientExecutive Board
Duration1 week

Problem

With 8 board members and 15+ applicants needing to align schedules for interviews, coordinating everyone's availability manually risked double-bookings and conflicts within a tight one-week window.

Solution

I managed the full interview scheduling process, collecting availability from all 8 board members, setting up a Calendly system connected to Google Calendar, and giving applicants a 3-day window to confirm their slot.

Result

Zero scheduling conflicts across 15+ applicant interviews. All interviews completed within the one-week window, with no manual back-and-forth needed.

"Having you as our Digital Manager made everything so much easier. You didn't just handle our digital platforms and inquiries, you also kept our interview process organized and on track. Everything went smoothly, with no scheduling conflicts at all. Thank you for handling this so well." — Virla, Director of Media
Case Study · 02
How I managed customer inquiries and order tracking across multiple channels.
ClientMSC / Landbank
Duration1 yr 6 mos

Problem

Members and clients had constant questions — membership status, account access, event details, and merchandise orders — with no single person managing the flow of inquiries.

Solution

I built an organized tracking system to log information, making it easier to track and manage incoming inquiries, then handled them directly across multiple channels — guiding members through membership and account requirements, and answering order-related questions covering pre-order status, delivery, and payment confirmation.

Result

All inquiries handled consistently and accurately, with no missed or unanswered messages across the full inquiry flow — backed by that same organized tracking system and well-maintained documentation.
